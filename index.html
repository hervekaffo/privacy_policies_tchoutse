Privacy Policy for Tchoutse

Effective date: March 18, 2026

Tchoutse ("we," "our," or "us") provides a mobile application that helps users discover and participate in events, view locations on maps, manage profiles, and receive notifications.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

1. Information We Collect

a. Account Information

When you create an account or sign in, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, profile photo, and authentication identifiers.

b. Location Information

If you allow location permissions, we may collect approximate and precise location data to show nearby events, support maps and navigation, and help verify event or venue check-ins when applicable.

c. User Content

We may collect content you choose to upload or submit, such as profile photos, event-related content, messages, and other information you provide in the app.

d. Device and Technical Information

We may automatically collect limited technical information such as device type, operating system, app version, crash data, diagnostic data, and security/performance logs.

e. Notification Data

If you enable notifications, we may use device tokens or similar identifiers to send push notifications.

2. How We Use Information

We use collected information to provide, operate, and improve Tchoutse; create and manage accounts; display maps, events, and location-based features; enable communication and app functionality; send notifications and service-related messages; maintain security; prevent fraud; troubleshoot issues; and comply with legal obligations.

3. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share information with service providers that help us operate the app, such as providers for hosting, authentication, storage, analytics, cloud media management, mapping, and notifications. We may also share information when required by law, to protect rights and safety, or as part of a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets.

4. Third-Party Services

Tchoutse may use third-party services such as Firebase, Google Maps Platform, Cloudinary, and Expo / notification-related services. These providers may process information under their own privacy policies.

5. Location Permissions

Tchoutse requests location permissions to support nearby event discovery, maps, navigation, and check-in features. You can disable location access at any time in your device settings, but some features may not work properly.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to provide the app and its features, maintain business and security records, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, update, or delete your information, and to disable permissions such as location or notifications.

9. Children's Privacy

Tchoutse is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. International Data Transfers

Your information may be processed and stored in countries other than your own, where data protection laws may differ.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the effective date above and may provide additional notice within the app or by other appropriate means.
